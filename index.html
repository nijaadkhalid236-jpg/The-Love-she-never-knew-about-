Chapter One — It Started Quietly

I never planned to fall for her.

There was no moment where I decided, “Today, I'm going to love this girl.”

It just happened.

Little by little.

A conversation here. A message there. A laugh that stayed in my head longer than it should have.

At first, she was simply someone I enjoyed talking to.

I didn't know that those small moments would eventually become memories I would carry with me.

Chapter Two — Hope

Sometimes hope begins with something so small that you don't even realize it is there.

A message. A smile. A little attention.

And before you know it, your heart starts imagining possibilities that were never promised.

I held onto that hope quietly.
